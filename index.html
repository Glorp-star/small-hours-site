GOOD TO KNOW

A few fair questions.

What does “one page” mean?

One scrollable page with your key business details, services, and contact or booking links. It isn’t an online store or a multi-page website.

Do I have to pay for a domain or hosting?

Those are separate services. You choose and own the accounts, and any extra cost should be agreed on before the site is published.

Will the site bring me more customers?

No website can promise that. The goal is to make your information easier to find and give visitors a clear next step.
